Privacy Policy for PDF Toolbox

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Toolbox - Split, Merge & Compress ("we", "the app") handles user data.

1. Zero Data Collection

PDF Toolbox does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, location data, or usage telemetry. We operate with a strict zero-knowledge architecture.

2. 100% On-Device Processing

All PDF file manipulations (splitting, merging, scaling, canvas standardization, and compression) are executed purely inside your local device's memory. No document content, pages, images, or metadata are ever uploaded to any cloud server or third party.

3. Zero Internet Permission

The Android application does not request or use the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It runs completely offline and cannot communicate over the network.

4. No Third-Party Trackers or Advertising

PDF Toolbox contains no ads, no trackers, no profiling mechanisms, and no third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy statement, please contact the developer via GitHub: PDF-Split-And-Merge-Tool Repository.
