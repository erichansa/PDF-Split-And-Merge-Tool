### Full Description (Up to 4,000 characters)

Looking for a truly private, fast, and lightweight PDF toolkit? **PDF Toolbox** is designed for students, professionals, and privacy-conscious users who want complete control over their documents. 

Whether dealing with sensitive contracts, financial statements, scanned invoices, or personal IDs, every single page is processed **100% locally on your device**. No cloud uploads, no external servers, and zero data leakage.

#### 🌟 Key Features

##### ✂️ 1. Flexible PDF Splitter
- **Split All Pages**: Extract every single page into an individual PDF file in seconds.
- **Custom Page Ranges**: Select and extract arbitrary ranges like `1-3, 5, 7-10` with smart punctuation handling.
- **Instant Preview**: Fast local processing with real-time feedback.

##### 📑 2. Smart Merger with Canvas Normalization (Exclusive!)
- **Reorder & Merge**: Drag and arrange multiple PDF files in any desired order.
- **Canvas Standardization (Fix inconsistent page sizes)**:
  - 🌟 **Fit to Standard A4 (Recommended)**: Automatically scales and centers phone photos, oversized scans, and irregular pages into clean, uniform A4 sheets.
  - **Match First Page**: Dynamically harmonizes all following pages with the first page's aspect ratio.
  - **Original Dimensions**: Preserves the native dimensions of each file.
- **Integrated Compression**: Compresses raster images on-the-fly to prevent merged PDFs from bloating in size.

##### 🗜️ 3. Dedicated Single-File Compressor
- **Standalone Slimming**: Easily compress individual large PDFs without needing to create a merge project.
- **3 Balanced Profiles**:
  - 🌟 **Smart HD Compress**: Resamples high-res scans to 150 DPI JPEG; shrinks file sizes by 70% to 90% while keeping text and visuals crisp.
  - 🚀 **Extreme Email Compress**: Optimizes pages to 100 DPI for strict email attachments and portal upload limits.
  - 🎨 **Lossless Cleanup**: Strips orphaned streams and metadata without altering visuals.
- **Optional Canvas Fix**: Normalize irregular pages during the compression process.
